Focused investor engagement for global companies seeking long‑term UK shareholders.

Capital Engagement Partners is an independent advisory firm working with listed companies worldwide to deepen and strengthen their relationships with London‑based institutional investors. The emphasis is on substance: understanding the questions that matter to professional investors and preparing management teams for the discussions that shape long‑term shareholding decisions.

View the Investor Engagement Programme

Complementing existing IR and brokers

Many companies already have strong investor relations teams and experienced corporate brokers. Our role is to complement that work by focusing on the specific issues UK institutions seek to understand before committing to long‑term ownership.

From presentation to real dialogue

There is often a difference between presenting a company and genuinely addressing what institutional investors are trying to discover. We help management teams prepare for the conversations that go beyond formal scripts and slide decks.

Global companies, UK capital

We work with global biopharma, industrial and other listed companies seeking to broaden or strengthen their UK institutional investor base, with a particular focus on London‑based long‑term mandates.

Core proposition

  • Independent investor engagement advisory for listed companies
  • Preparation for meetings with UK institutional investors
  • Structured identification of key investor questions
  • Programmes of one‑to‑one meetings in London
  • Feedback and recommendations following investor discussions
  • Designed to complement existing IR and broker relationships
